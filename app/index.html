66 straight months of trade surplus

Exports reached US$234.04bn in Jan–Oct 2025 (+6.96% yoy) against US$198.16bn of imports — a US$35.88bn surplus, the longest streak since May 2020. China, the US and India took 41.8% of non-oil exports.

Human development keeps climbing

The Human Development Index (IPM) reached 75.02 in 2024, up 0.63 points on 2023 — the fifth straight annual rise. Life expectancy hit 74.15 years, but the gap is wide: DKI Jakarta scores 83.08 while Highland Papua scores 53.42.

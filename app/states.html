Sources & methodology

    Regional datasets

      Methodology

      • Observation years are kept honest: poverty, income and Gini are 2024; population and internet are 2025. A 2024 figure is never relabelled 2025.
      • National benchmarks are not averages of the sixteen regions: median income (RM7,017), poverty (5.1%) and internet (97.1%) come from the national publications.
      • Kuala Lumpur and Putrajaya are fully urban. Where a rural figure does not exist for them, it is shown as not available — never as 0%.
      • Household and individual internet differ: household access (97.1%) counts households; individual use (98.3%) counts people aged 15+ who used the internet in the last 3 months. Never merged.
      • The “Malaysia” row in the OpenDOSM internet file is a national benchmark, not a seventeenth state.
      • Tile positions are schematic. The cartogram places states in approximate geographic order; it is not a survey-accurate map.

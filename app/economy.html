Jobs

Open unemployment was 4.85% in August 2025 (7.46 million people), down from 4.91% a year earlier — but up from 4.76% in February 2025. The pain sits with the young: unemployment among 15–24-year-olds was 16.9%. Nearly a quarter of workers (24.77%) work part-time and 7.91% are underemployed. Average employee wage: Rp3.33m a month (Aug 2025, Sakernas).

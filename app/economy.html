Real GDP grew 5.2% in 2025 — the same as 2024 — with nominal GDP at RM2.03 trillion; inflation cooled to 1.4% and unemployment to 3.0%.

Sector growth, 2025

Real growth by sector, % year on year. The dashed line is national GDP growth (5.2%). Source: DOSM, Gross Domestic Product 2025 (15 May 2026).

Only Construction (12.2%) and Services (5.4%) grew faster than GDP itself. Sector growth is not sector share of GDP.

Inflation cooled

Annual average CPI, %. Source: DOSM, Analysis of Annual CPI 2025 (29 Apr 2026).

Inflation fell 0.4 percentage points, from 1.8% in 2024 to 1.4% in 2025.

Unemployment edged down

Annual average unemployment rate, %. Source: DOSM, Labour Force Survey 2025 (26 Jun 2026).

Unemployment fell 0.2 percentage points, from 3.2% in 2024 to 3.0% in 2025.

What the economy is made of

Share of nominal GDP, 2025. Source: DOSM, Gross Domestic Product 2025 (15 May 2026).

Services and manufacturing account for 82.5% of GDP; all other sectors make up the remaining 17.5%.

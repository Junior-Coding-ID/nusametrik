Methodology

Cut-off rule. A figure appears here only if its official release was published on or before 26 November 2026. Where a full-year 2025 release did not exist yet, the site shows the latest published value and labels it with its true period — never a forecast presented as fact.

Rounding. Provincial populations are BPS mid-2025 estimates rounded to the nearest 100 in the source table; their sum (284.44m) matches the national estimate. Densities are the published mid-2025 values. National density (≈150/km²) is computed from the national population and land area (1,890,179.8 km²).

Island groups. Provinces are grouped by the seven BPS geographical units; the Explorer filter uses four macro groups (Sumatra, Java, Kalimantan, Eastern Indonesia) for simplicity.

Offline by design. All data lives in data.js — the site's only data source. No fetch, no CDN, no external font, no tracking, no cookies. Theme and language preferences are stored locally in your browser only.

Corrections. Found an error? Every figure above names its release — check it against the source and the site will be corrected. One wrong number loses the game; that is the standard this site holds itself to.

NUSAMETRIK — an independent statistics site. Sources: BPS (Statistics Indonesia), Bank Indonesia, UNDP, World Bank. Works fully offline.

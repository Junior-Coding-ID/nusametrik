Connectivity

Indonesia is going online fast — 77.63% of people aged 5+ used the internet in 2025, up from 62.10% in 2021. But the connection is uneven: cities are 12.58 points ahead of villages, and the physical grid still misses some households.

The digital climb

People using the internet, % of population aged 5+

BPS (Susenas / Statistik Telekomunikasi Indonesia). Growth averaged about 3.9 points a year from 2021 to 2025; the biggest single jump came in 2025 (+4.85 points).

The gap that will not close by itself

Urban users: 79.13% (2024) → 82.72% (2025). Rural users: 63.71% → 70.14%. The gap narrowed from 15.42 to 12.58 points in one year — rural growth (+6.43) outpaced urban growth (+3.59) — but 3 in 10 rural residents are still offline.

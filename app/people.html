Poverty fell

Absolute household poverty, %. Source: DOSM, Poverty in Malaysia 2024 (8 Oct 2025).

Poverty fell 1.1 percentage points, from 6.2% in 2022 to 5.1% in 2024, for households in the official Malaysian-citizen statistical coverage.

Inequality narrowed

Gini coefficient, 0–1 scale. Source: DOSM, Income Inequality 2024 (8 Oct 2025).

The Gini coefficient fell 0.014, from 0.404 in 2022 to 0.390 in 2024. Gini is a 0–1 index, not a percent.

The urban–rural internet gap

Households with internet access, 2025. Source: DOSM, ICT Use and Access by Individuals and Households Survey Report 2025 (23 Apr 2026).

Urban households 99.0%, rural 90.7% — a gap of 8.3 percentage points; national household figure 97.1%. Individual internet use (98.3%) counts people aged 15+ who used the internet in the last 3 months — a different measure, never merged.

Electricity access, 2024

Dwellings with electricity access, %. Source: DOSM, Basic Amenities Survey Report 2024 (24 Dec 2025).

Near-universal: 99.9% nationally, 100.0% urban, 99.4% rural.
